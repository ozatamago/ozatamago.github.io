Recommendation letters

Prof. Chong Liu · Updated: Nov 11, 2026

Thank you for agreeing to write for me. I am applying to 9 PhD programs for Fall 2027 entry. This page collects the deadlines, how submission works, and the materials. It is not listed on the site or in search engines; only people I send the link to are directed here.

What I am asking

  • One English letter, on institutional letterhead, signed and dated, works for every program.
  • Each school emails you its own submission link from its application system. It would help if you could prepare the letter before those emails arrive.
  • Some schools also ask rating questions on the submission page (see “Notes for recommenders” below).
  • “Please submit by” is about one week before each deadline; happy to adjust to your schedule.

How submission works

  1. I register you as a recommender in each school's application system (registration date to be confirmed).
  2. Each school emails you a link for that school only. It may land in your spam folder.
  3. Upload the letter at the link, answer any questions, and submit. I cannot see the letters.
  4. I waive my right to see the letters.
  5. I will also track the status in each system and let you know before a deadline if anything is missing.
