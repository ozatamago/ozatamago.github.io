I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science at Kwansei Gakuin University and a Research Part-timer II in the Laboratory for Biologically Inspired Computing (PI: Koichi Takahashi) at the RIKEN Center for Biosystems Dynamics Research (BDR), also affiliated with the RIKEN AGIS program. From August 2025 to May 2026, I was an exchange student in the Department of Computer Science at the University at Albany, SUNY, where I continue to work with Chong Liu.

My long-term goal is to create living organisms with arbitrary, desired functions. To pursue this vision, I am interested in laboratory automation, including automated experiment execution and automated experimental design/planning.

Outside research, I practice Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (blue belt) and enjoy reading and running.

Research interests

My research is on Bayesian optimization, bandits, and active learning for self-driving laboratories, and on the infrastructure that lets AI agents operate laboratory instruments. Recently I have been working on:

  1. Machine learning on experimental protocols: OFML and ProtocolUCB [R1] [W1]
  2. Cumulative regret analysis of Lipschitz bandits [R2]
  3. Structured planning for LLM agents [R3]
  4. Laboratory automation and experiment infrastructure
  5. Quantum algorithms for Gaussian-process Bayesian optimization

Details of each project are on the Research Experience page; papers and talks are listed on Publications.
